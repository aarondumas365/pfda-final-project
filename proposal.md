## Description
The RNG Combat simulator is a turn-based fighting game where the player has to defeat waves of randomized enemies. This program is relevant to the media and digital arts sphere because it is an interactive experience in the form of a video game, and it features art and animations.

## Features
- RNG Element
	- Each attack made by the player and enemy is randomized. Enemies' stats and moves are also randomly generated.
- Battle Waves
	- Each battle is a wave; if the player successfully defeats the enemy, they move on to the next round. If the player is defeated, it's game over.
- Turn-Based Combat
	- The player and the enemy take turns attacking or defending each other.

## Challenges
- Creating a game loop that encourages the player to beat their high score.
- Creating a proper turn-based system within the gameplay.
- Making sure the random elements don't give the player a disadvantage.
